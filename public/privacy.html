# ClearSky Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 September 2026**

ClearSky (“ClearSky”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides AI-powered creative tools for generating websites, logos, flyers, app and game concepts, image content, and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClearSky may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ClearSky, we may collect:

- Account information such as your email address and account credentials.
- Verification information such as one-time verification codes sent to your email.
- Subscription information such as your selected plan, billing cycle, subscription status, and payment channel.
- Transaction information such as payment references, amounts, currency, status, and payment provider information.
- Prompts, instructions, uploaded content, and other material you submit to ClearSky for AI generation.
- Usage information such as the tools you use and generation records.
- Technical information reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service.

ClearSky does not intentionally display your password in your profile. Payment card details are handled by the applicable payment provider rather than displayed by ClearSky.

## 2. How we use information

We may use information to:

- Create and maintain your account.
- Authenticate and verify account ownership.
- Provide AI generation and other ClearSky features.
- Process and verify subscriptions and payments.
- Manage Premium applications.
- Provide customer support.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, improve, and monitor ClearSky.
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

## 3. AI services

When you submit a prompt or other content to an AI feature, that information may be transmitted to the AI service provider used by ClearSky to process the request. ClearSky should configure these integrations according to the applicable provider's terms and privacy settings.

Do not submit confidential information, passwords, payment credentials, or other sensitive information into an AI prompt unless the relevant feature specifically requires it and you are authorized to provide it.

## 4. Payments

ClearSky may use third-party payment processors, such as Paystack, to process payments.

ClearSky does not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers in its application database. We may receive and store transaction references, payment status, amount, currency, and payment channel as necessary to confirm and manage a transaction.

## 5. Email and OTP verification

We may use your email address to send:

- Account verification codes.
- Login verification codes.
- Security notices.
- Transactional messages.
- Premium application notifications or account-related communications.

## 6. Premium applications

If you apply for Premium, your application information may be reviewed by authorized ClearSky administrators. Application decisions and related administrative records may be retained for account and service-management purposes.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

ClearSky may use cookies, local storage, session tokens, or similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain security, remember preferences, and operate the application.

Users should be given appropriate controls for non-essential cookies where required by applicable law.

## 8. Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate ClearSky, including hosting, email, authentication, AI, payment, security, analytics, and infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, or a lawful request, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of ClearSky, its users, or others.

We do not sell passwords or payment-card credentials.

## 9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information, including server-side handling of sensitive credentials, authentication controls, access controls, and payment verification.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use a strong, unique password and should not share verification codes or account credentials.

## 10. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the service, maintaining account records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, complying with legal obligations, and enforcing agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which can include rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other forms of control over your information.

To make a privacy request, contact the ClearSky customer-care/privacy contact shown in the application.

## 12. Children's privacy

ClearSky is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where age-related consent or parental authorization is legally required, ClearSky should obtain it before processing the relevant information.

## 13. International processing

Some service providers used by ClearSky may process information in countries outside Nigeria. Where applicable, ClearSky should use appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable requirements for international transfers.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ClearSky's services, technology, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

## 15. Contact

For privacy or customer-care questions, use the customer-care email configured in the ClearSky application.
